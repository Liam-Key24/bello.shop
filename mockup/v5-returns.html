Returns & Refunds

Not happy? We'll make it right. Guaranteed.

🔄 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Changed your mind? No problem. You have 30 days from delivery to return any item for a full refund. No questions asked.

All we ask is that the product is unused, in its original packaging, and in resaleable condition.

📋 How to Return an Item

  1. 1
    Contact Us
    Email us at returns@bello.shop with your order number and reason for return.
  2. 2
    Get Your Return Label
    We'll email you a prepaid return label within 24 hours.
  3. 3
    Pack & Send
    Pack the item securely, attach the return label, and drop it at any post office.
  4. 4
    Get Your Refund
    Once we receive your return, we'll process your refund within 3-5 business days.

💷 Refund Policy

Full refund: You'll receive a full refund to your original payment method, including the original shipping cost (if the return is our fault).

Processing time: Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days of receiving your return. It may take an additional 5-7 days for the refund to appear in your account, depending on your bank.

Return shipping: Free prepaid label provided for all returns.

🔧 Damaged or Defective Items

We'll replace it immediately. If you receive a damaged or defective item, contact us right away with photos. We'll send a replacement at no charge, or issue a full refund if you prefer.

You don't need to return the damaged item unless we ask you to.

❓ Questions?

Need help with a return? Get in touch and we'll sort it out quickly.
