I blended inspiration from two sources:

**Festival Poster Vibes:**
- Bold sunny yellow (#E8E661) for accent and energy
- Confident, friendly tone
- Simple badge icons (🌿 ♻️ ⭐)

**Premium Skincare Aesthetic:**
- Huge, clean headlines

**Result:** A site that feels both **premium** (like high-end skincare) and **approachable** (like a friendly eco brand). Not cheap dropshipping vibes, not preachy eco-warrior either—just honest, quality, sustainable products.

### Color Palette
- **Sun Yellow (#E8E661):** Badges, highlights, optimism
- **Sage Green (#98A65D):** Main buttons, trust elements
- **Deep Teal (#2D4654):** Secondary buttons, footer
- **Warm Cream (#FBF8F3):** Background (soft, premium)
- **Charcoal (#1A1A1A):** Text (readable, modern)

### Typography
- **Headlines:** 72px, thin, airy (like "Serum" skincare brands)

## AutoDS Integration: What You Need to Know

**What AutoDS Does:**
- Automatically imports products from suppliers
- Syncs inventory in real-time (stock levels, prices)
- Fulfills orders automatically when customers buy

**How It Works with Your Site:**
1. You set up AutoDS + Shopify app
2. Import your 2 products into AutoDS
3. AutoDS syncs them to your Shopify store
4. Your site pulls product data from Shopify (like it does now)
5. When someone buys, AutoDS fulfills the order automatically

**What Changes on the Frontend:**
- Nothing! Your site talks to Shopify, Shopify talks to AutoDS
- Product data, inventory, and prices sync automatically
- No code changes needed for basic AutoDS functionality

**Testing Checklist (Phase 6):**
- Import 2 test products via AutoDS
- Verify they appear correctly on your site
- Place a test order
- Confirm order sent to AutoDS for fulfillment
- Test inventory sync (product goes out of stock)
- Test price sync (supplier price changes)
