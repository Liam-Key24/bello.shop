Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us.

🚚 When will my order arrive?

Standard UK delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available at checkout. Orders placed before 2pm are dispatched same day.

🔄 What's your return policy?

30-day money-back guarantee. Return any unused item in its original packaging for a full refund. We provide free prepaid return labels.

🌍 Are your products really eco-friendly?

Yes! Every product is eco-certified, made from sustainable materials, and produced ethically. We also offset 100% of our shipping emissions and use plastic-free packaging.

💷 Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! Free standard shipping on all UK orders over £30. Orders under £30 have a £3.99 shipping fee.

📦 How is my order packaged?

100% plastic-free! We use recycled cardboard boxes, paper tape, and biodegradable packing materials. Everything is recyclable or compostable.

✓ What if my item arrives damaged?

We'll replace it immediately at no charge. Contact us with photos and we'll send a replacement right away.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal via our secure Shopify checkout.

📧 How can I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order is dispatched. Use it to track your parcel in real-time.

❓ Still have questions?

Get in touch and we'll help you out.
