Shipping & Delivery

Fast, reliable, carbon-neutral delivery to your door.

🚚 Delivery Times

Location Standard Delivery Express Delivery
UK Mainland 3-5 business days 1-2 business days
Northern Ireland 5-7 business days 2-3 business days
Scottish Highlands & Islands 5-7 business days 3-4 business days

Orders placed before 2pm Monday-Friday are dispatched the same day. Weekend orders are processed Monday morning.

💷 Shipping Costs

Standard Delivery: FREE on orders over £30. £3.99 for orders under £30.

Express Delivery: £7.99 (available at checkout)

Saturday Delivery: £9.99 (order before Thursday 2pm)

💚 Carbon Neutral Shipping: We offset 100% of our delivery emissions at no extra cost to you.

📦 Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your parcel in real-time using our shipping partner's website.

If you haven't received tracking information within 24 hours of your order confirmation, please contact us.

📋 Packaging

100% Plastic-Free: All our packaging is recyclable or compostable. We use recycled cardboard boxes, paper tape, and biodegradable packing materials.

Minimal Waste: We only use what's necessary to protect your products during transit. No excess packaging, no plastic fillers.

Questions about your delivery? Check our FAQ or get in touch.
